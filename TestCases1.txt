Lab 1: Part 2 Test Case
A public library has branches. Each branch has a unique BranchID, name, and address. A branch employs many librarians, and each librarian works at exactly one branch. A librarian has a unique LibrarianID, name, email, and multiple phone numbers.


The library has books identified by ISBN, title, publication year, and one or more authors. A book may have multiple physical copies. A copy is weak: it is identified by its owning book together with a CopyNumber, which is a partial key. Each copy has a condition and an acquisition date. The identifying relationship between Book and Copy must be represented explicitly.


Members have a unique MemberID, name, email, and address. Members can borrow copies, and a copy can be borrowed by different members over time. Each borrowing event records BorrowDate, DueDate, and optional ReturnDate. A librarian processes each borrowing event.


A supplier supplies a particular book to a particular branch. This is a ternary relationship among Supplier, Book, and Branch, with attributes SupplyDate and Quantity. A supplier has a unique SupplierID, name, and contact email. A branch may receive the same book from multiple suppliers. Preserve the ternary relationship rather than splitting it into binary relationships.
